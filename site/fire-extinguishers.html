Services we offer

Services we offer

Condon Fire & Safety provides commercial fire extinguisher, kitchen suppression-system, and emergency-lighting inspection, maintenance, repair, and compliance support in Indiana.

Inspection & maintenance

The right service for every stage of your life-safety equipment’s life.

Fire extinguisher inspection

Routine inspections help identify equipment that needs service and give your team a clearer picture of what is in place.

Kitchen suppression systems

Inspection and maintenance support for commercial kitchen suppression systems helps keep your cooking operation prepared for the unexpected.

Emergency lighting inspection

We inspect exit signs and emergency lighting to help ensure these essential systems are ready when normal power is interrupted.

Fire hydrant inspections

Routine hydrant inspections help identify visible issues, support reliable access for fire response, and keep your property's safety equipment on schedule.

Fire safety classes

We offer hands-on fire safety training so your staff knows how to respond confidently — covering proper extinguisher use, class types, and when to evacuate.

Recharge, replacement & compliance

When equipment has been used, requires attention, or needs replacement, we provide practical next-step support and service guidance.
