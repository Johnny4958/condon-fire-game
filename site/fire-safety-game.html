Fire Safety Game

Spot the hazards. Put out the fire.

A free 3D game from Condon Fire & Safety. Search each room for the fire hazards a trained inspector would catch, then pick a working extinguisher and stop the fire before it spreads.

Condon Fire Safety GameWorks on phones and computers · Turn your phone sideways for the best view
Play full screen

On a phone, full screen gives you the most room to look around.

How to play

Three rooms. One timer. Every hazard counts.

01

Spot the hazards

You get a minute to search each room. Tag what's wrong: blocked exits, covered smoke detectors, overloaded outlets, greasy rags and more. The hazards change every game.

02

Check your extinguisher

Pick the extinguisher rated for what's burning, and check its gauge and inspection tag before you grab it. A bad one won't help you when the fire starts.

03

Put out the fire

Stay low under the smoke, use the kitchen and paint booth suppression systems, and put out all three fires to unlock harder mode.

Controls

Play on a phone or a computer.

Laptop or desktop

Move with the keyboard and look around with the mouse. Press C, Q or left Ctrl to crouch under the smoke.

Phone or tablet

Use the on-screen controls to move and look, and the Crouch button to get under the smoke. Turn your phone sideways for the best view.
